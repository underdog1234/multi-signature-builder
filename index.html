How to use it

Thunderbird

  1. Click Download signature (.html) and save it somewhere permanent.
  2. Open Account Settings and pick your email account.
  3. Tick Attach the signature from a file and choose the file you saved.

Outlook or Gmail

  1. Click Copy signature.
  2. Open the signature settings and paste it into the signature box.

Picture signature

  1. Click Download signature picture (.png).
  2. In your email app's signature box, insert the picture (look for the picture icon).

A picture can't have clickable links, so use the text version where you can. For the text version, the logo is loaded from this website, so it shows for anyone you email. Some email apps hide pictures until the reader allows them.
